# facilities-data-repo

US hospitals, as data for the oceansensing.org ocean map: every point of the
National Structures Dataset's *Hospitals/Medical Centers*, with its name and
street address, and — where the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services'
*Hospital General Information* lists it — its phone, hospital type and whether
it runs an emergency department.

Updated daily; the sources are read each month.

## Sources and credits

**The places are the U.S. Geological Survey's**: The National Map's National Structures Dataset (the service's credit *"USGS TNM – National Structures Dataset"*; The National Map's data free and in the public domain, its credit *"Map services and data available from U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program"*). **What a hospital offers is CMS's**: *Hospital General Information*, data.cms.gov's provider data (no license statement found on its page, its catalog's FAQ or its footer, so its use rests on its being a federal work, 17 U.S.C. § 105).
